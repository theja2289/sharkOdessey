# Shark Odyssey

A true-facts shark adventure game. Play it at https://theja2289.github.io/sharkOdessey/

📱 **Publishing to Google Play:** follow the step-by-step guide in [ANDROID.md](ANDROID.md).

## Realistic look and Explore mode

The title screen has a Cartoon / Realistic switch, a Detail setting (Auto, Low, Medium, High) and an **Explore the ocean** mode where you swim the shark freely through a 3D ocean. The realistic water lighting is adapted from [Abyssal Garden](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden) by Maksymilian Dendura (MIT License).
