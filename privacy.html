Shark Odyssey — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Shark Odyssey is an educational shark game. We built it to be safe for children.

What we collect

Nothing. The game doesn't ask for your name, email, location, or any other personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no in-app purchases.

Data on your device

The game keeps no record of who you are. If your phone saves a copy of the game for offline play, that copy stays on your phone and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Internet use

The game plays offline. If you're connected, it loads public shark photos and short descriptions from Wikipedia. When that happens, your device contacts Wikipedia's servers the way a web browser would, and Wikipedia's own privacy policy applies. The game sends Wikipedia nothing about you.

Children

We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/theja2289/sharkOdessey.
